Admissions FAQ

Questions, answered.

When is the application deadline?+

Fall applications close on January 15. Early action applicants receive a decision within four weeks.

Do you offer scholarships?+

Yes. More than 70% of students receive merit or need-based aid, and every applicant is automatically considered.

Can international students apply?+

Absolutely. Students from over 60 countries study here, with dedicated visa and housing support from your first day.

What is the student-to-teacher ratio?+

Up to 38 students per teacher.
